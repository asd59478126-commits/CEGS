# CEGS Research Status

This directory is the temporal and research-status index for CEGS.

CEGS does not interpret all documents through a single "latest version." Instead, it preserves several layers at the same time:

1. **Current theory:** Construction theory structures currently in use or maintained.
2. **Versioned documents:** Documents with explicit historical dates, version context, or content later superseded by subsequent research.
3. **Ongoing research:** Questions that are not finalized and are still being derived, evaluated, or awaiting validation.
4. **Historical experiments:** Early Qualification / Stage 1 experiments and their audit materials.
5. **Date evidence:** Original materials formed on specific research dates; a date directory is not a substitute for theoretical classification.

## New Cross-Layer Mapping Direction

Added 2026-09-12:

```text
Internal State
      ↓
External Representation
      ↓
Interpretation
```

Current research position:

- `Internal State ≠ External Representation`
- `External Representation ≠ Interpretation`
- Representation is not treated as transparent transmission.
- Understanding is not treated as direct retrieval of the original state.
- The research focus is thematic continuity under changes of form.

This is a cross-layer structural supplement to Construction research. It is not provisionally treated as a new fifteenth core item, nor is it claimed to be a complete ontology of mind or consciousness.

## Interpretation Rules

A newer document is not necessarily theoretically more correct; an older document is not necessarily invalid.

Dates preserve formation history. Research status represents current standing. Neither can replace the other.

`00_INDEX/CONTENT_CATALOG.md` is the shared entry catalog for humans and AI; `00_INDEX/AI_INGESTION_GUIDE.md` defines the rules that prevent AI readers from flattening the data hierarchy.
