## Reading architecture

```text
                    CEGS
                      │
          ┌───────────┼───────────┐
          ↓           ↓           ↓
     Human / 中文  Human / EN   AI / EN
          │           │           │
          └───────────┼───────────┘
                      ↓
             Current Research Core
                      ↓
        Theory / Active Research / History
```

The three entrances are different reading interfaces over the same research archive. They do not represent three different versions of the theory.

### Language rule

English is the primary machine-readable research language for the repository's AI-facing layer.

Chinese remains important as a human-to-model communication layer in the author's working process, especially where the author's Chinese phrasing carries precise local distinctions or context. The Chinese entrance therefore remains a first-class human reading interface rather than a machine-ingestion substitute.

### Preservation rule

The three entrances do not replace historical records. Current theory, active research, versioned materials, dated evidence, and historical experiments remain separated and preserved.

For detailed repository structure and cross-file navigation, use the secondary indexes under `00_INDEX/` after entering through one of the three interfaces.

## Current research identity

CEGS is the research carrier, not Construction itself.

Construction currently studies how necessary structure can be established, preserved, traced, and re-qualified when multidimensional information cannot be retained in full, with current emphasis on adaptive construction and thematic invariance.

```text
Construction ≠ CEGS
MCS ≠ CEGS Stage 1
Qualification ≠ Construction
```

See the three entries above before reading the historical and engineering layers.
