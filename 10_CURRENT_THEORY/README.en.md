# 10_CURRENT_THEORY

Working area for the current Construction theory.

This directory contains only documents that are still adopted, currently in use, or serve as current theoretical baselines. Status is not determined by document date alone.

## foundation/

Core theoretical documents for the Construction Framework and the conditions under which Construction units are established and represented.

- `foundation/construction-framework.md`: Original Construction framework foundation; preserves the TOKN, Direction, Invariant, Alignment, Order, Narrative Chain, Traceability, Memory re-entry, and Qualification evolution lines.
- `foundation/構築（Construction）：多維資訊概念中的適應性結構與主題性不變.md`: The current main Construction theory; the 2026-09-12 update added the “State → Representation → Interpretation” cross-layer mapping.
- `foundation/目前研究核心第一版：內容資料正式紀錄.md`: Formal content consolidation of the first core version, maintaining the baseline of fourteen core candidates.

## formalization/

Current type-first formalization work. These documents may still contain unresolved type, semantic, or definitional conflicts; therefore, being located under `current` does not mean being proven.

## appendices/

Local derivations and appendices supporting the current theory, such as the relativity of expansion and the necessity of anchors.

## Important Principles

When newer research changes an older definition, historical documents are not overwritten. The new work is marked as the current version, while the evolutionary relationship is preserved in the research record.

The original dated materials from 2026-09-10 and 2026-09-12 remain in their respective date directories as temporal and version evidence; dated materials do not directly replace the current-theory classification.
